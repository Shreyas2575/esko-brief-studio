# 📦 Packaging Design Brief: Design Agency Brief: Dancing Fish Granola - Berry Crunch (480g Carton Remediation)

**Executive Summary:** This production brief authorizes the comprehensive regulatory, nutritional, and artwork remediation of Dancing Fish Granola - Berry Crunch (480g carton) for the United States retail market. The packaging dieline has undergone a pre-press compliance audit reconciling severe discrepancies between the previous artwork PDF and specification sheet. Key directives include aligning to US FDA 21 CFR labeling standards by declaring dual-unit net quantity on the Principal Display Panel (PDP), restructuring the Nutrition Facts panel to an 8-serving (60g RACC) format, removing conflicting claims ('Certified Vegan' and 'No Added Sugar' badges due to the presence of honey and coconut sugar), updating allergen statements to FALCPA/FASTER Act standards, and rectifying technical prepress errors including spelling typos and guide-mark separations.
